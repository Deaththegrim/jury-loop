# Jury: idle loop

Jury, one of my Voyage characters, as a seamless idle loop: a calm game-style idle stance, slow
breathing through the chest and shoulder plates, and the long red coat moving behind his legs
while his fists stay in front of it. Made locally with LTX-2.5 (first and last frame both the
portrait, prompted as a video game idle animation), then the tail blended into the first frame
so the wrap doesn't pop.

![Jury idle loop](jury-loop-4s.webp)

Files:
- `jury-loop-4s.webp`: the loop, 768x1024, 24 fps, loops forever, 4.05 MB
- `jury-loop-4s.mp4`: the same loop at 48 fps
- `jury-raw-4s-with-audio.mp4`: the untouched render with its wind and breath sound (not looped)
- `jury-source.png`: the portrait it starts and ends on (768x1024)
